#About Me
My name is Taylor Rockefeller, and I am pursuing a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering with a concentration in Motorsports. I have always had a passion for motorsports since childhood, but my career path so far has taken me down different paths that have eventually led me to pursue my passion. Initially I spent time in the Marine Corps and learned about explosive principles to both construct and deconstruct obstacles. Along with this I rose through the ranks and learned many leadership lessons about how to lead and how to follow. After my time in service, I spent time in the oil and gas industry before beginning my current work which consists of bridge construction. This has given me years of hands-on experience working with and reading construction plans similar to those in my previous drafting courses. Throughout this career journey, I have learned that I have not only a passion for motorsports but also for problem solving and design/construction. And though I am more than capable of working on my own, I enjoy being part of a team of capable individuals with a shared passion. This is why I feel motorsports engineering is the perfect career choice for me. Though I am still studying to become an engineer, I have had many events in my professional career where I have had to make a decision that had to be justified to higher positions. Though these decisions were made to the best of my ability in the given situation; there are many times where your leadership may have more experience in such a situation and may provide you with a better alternative. There are also times you decision can be "right" but may not fit into a larger plan that you are not fully aware of. This is why you must be able to walk someone through your reasoning for using a certain method or why you did no use another, but you must also be willing to listen to a fresh perspective or someone more experienced.
